Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

Welcome to Right Routes. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

When you use our App, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Email address and profile information you provide during registration.
  • Location Data: Real-time location data (GPS) to provide route tracking and planning features.
  • Health & Fitness Data: Running stats, pace, distance, and calories burned.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our App.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services.
  • To track your runs and create routes.
  • To manage team leaderboards and history.
  • To communicate with you regarding updates and support.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following cases:

  • With your consent (e.g., sharing routes or leaderboard stats).
  • To comply with legal obligations.
  • With service providers who assist us in operating our App.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Your Choices

You can control location access and biometric login (Touch ID) through your device settings. You may also update or delete your account information at any time within the App.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@rightroutes.com.
